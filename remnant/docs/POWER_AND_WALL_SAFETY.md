# Power progression and safe movement

Survival enemy health, damage and tempo now depend on wave number, not hero level. Wave 30 no longer secretly becomes harder when Elior levels up. Boss Gauntlet keeps its separate entry-strength rules.

Each hero level contributes 10 base health and equipment attack growth. Attack growth is +3% per level through level 100, then 35% of that rate. Strength adds base attack and a multiplier; its bonus softens above 60, as do Agility and Wisdom. Damage reduction caps at 90%, critical chance at 75%, attack speed at ×3 and parry timing at 600 ms. See `CONTROLLED_POWER_CURVE.md` for the current balance targets and measurements.

Honed Edge, Kinship and Lamp of the Word support 50 ranks, with +10% per rank for ranks 1–10, +5% for 11–25 and +2.5% for 26–50. Ability and Echo bonuses add together. Iron Skin supports 20 ranks; Regeneration, Radiance and Keen Eye support 10. Previously spent points and learned ranks remain intact. Character and Passive Skills offer 1/5/10/25/50-point batches. Character shows the result before spending; both screens save immediately. Prerequisites, point budgets and rank limits still apply.

Every newly completed Survival wave grants a random Legendary or Covenant item before autosaving and showing the blessing choice. The reward is already in Equipment; choosing a blessing is independent of the gear. Every fifth wave guarantees one of three weapon families. Other waves can grant weapons, helm, armor, gauntlets, boots, relic or ring. Two or three unique affixes add variation. Existing milestone trophies and contracts still award their separate prizes.

Reward Power is `floor(20 + level × 0.75 + wave × 10 + wave^1.35 × 1.2)` with a finite ceiling. At level 150, wave 1 awards Power 143, wave 5 Power 193, wave 30 Power 550, and wave 31 Power 566. Primary gear stats scale by `1 + Power × 0.02`; secondary modifiers grow more gently. Item names, completion messages and detail panels show Power. Repeated completion callbacks cannot award a cleared wave twice; replaying and clearing it during another run can earn a new roll. Existing items retain their old modifiers.

Sovereign damage starts at ×1.2 at level 100, reaches ×1.4 at level 150, then grows more gently. It adds to ability/super and Echo bonuses. Echo leadership and hero-attack inheritance grow more gently; Echoes retain their own levels, identities, role abilities and evolution rules. Combined gear and shadow life steal is limited to 4% of maximum health per second, using health actually removed from enemies. Survival damage continues increasing after wave 30. Each complete wave has an experience budget of 0.365–1.35 levels, shared across its enemies; XP bonuses can increase it.

Horizontal physics sweeps every crossed tile column, preventing fast lunges and knockback from skipping thin walls. Full-body tile checks replace sampled teleport destinations. Phantom Step revalidates its landing after the vanish, Judgment Cross uses safe blink positions, scripted finishers validate every placement, and Echo exchanges reject unsafe destinations. Executions require line of sight. Only clear ground is recorded as safe footing; a player embedded by an old position or changed geometry returns to valid safe footing when available. Normal room exits and one-way platforms retain their behavior.

Verification: `node --test scripts/power-walls.test.mjs` covers attribute gains, bulk learning, reward rolls/scaling/migration and swept physics in both directions at 120/60/10 FPS time steps. `node scripts/power-walls.mjs` tests the production build's real UI, level-150 wave-30 combat with a valid earned point budget, fixed-wave enemy strength across hero levels, wall-adjacent attacks/teleports/finishers/swaps, duplicate reward prevention, autosave and mobile layout. The existing combat, progression, visuals, controller, audio and wave-access suites remain release gates.
