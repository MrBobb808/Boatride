# Squad links, wave blessings and voice orders

- Summon a Mage + Fighter for **Frostbreak**, Fighter + Ninja for **Skyfall**, or Guardian + Healer for **Sanctuary**. Role special moves open a short link window. Both allies must be present, alive, nearby and reachable. Hold orders stay put; bosses keep their launch immunity. A participating Echo rests seven seconds between links. The Echoes menu explains each combination.
- Every cleared survival wave offers three distinct blessings. Six ranked bonuses stack up to five times; health, Energy and Balm are repeatable recovery choices. Reckless Oath explicitly increases incoming damage. Bonuses last for the current run and reset on death, leaving or loading a save. Permanent inventory and earned wave checkpoints are unchanged. Boss-only runs keep their existing recovery breaks.
- The microphone button beside the phone's Map button, **V** on a keyboard, or **Command → Voice orders** opens a paused command panel. Tap to speak; final speech results apply supported orders. Interim results never apply. The panel always includes typed commands and a visible Resume control. It stops recognition when closed, timed out, denied or backgrounded. It uses the browser's speech service, with no game API key, uploaded audio archive or language-model backend. Speech availability and network requirements depend on the browser; hardware recognition on an actual iPhone must be checked on-device.
- Examples: “Guardian, protect me and ninja, attack archers”; “Mage, control groups”; “Everyone, hold here”; “Summon two”; “Recall everyone”. Give up to four short clauses. Missing roles or unclear clauses reject the entire order. Ranged, boss and crowd preferences persist with the Echo, and ordinary orders clear those preferences.
- Elior's illustrated strike frame now follows each equipped move's contact window. Small planted-foot anticipation/contact/recovery adjustments also move the weapon attachment and trail. Heavy blows have distinct recoil; trails emphasize the active part of a swing. Existing hitstop buffering and defensive cancel windows are preserved.

Validation: production TypeScript/build and controller tests; `squad-systems.test.mjs` for offer caps, run reset, language rejection and contact timing; `squad-expansion.mjs` for real role combinations, wave choice UI, run effects, target selection, save persistence, desktop/phone layouts and speech lifecycle with a deterministic recognition mock. The existing combat, art, skills, progression, offline and wave-access suites remain release gates.
