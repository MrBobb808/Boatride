# Remnant: Ascension

A 2D side-scrolling biblical dark fantasy action RPG. You play Elior, the
weakest watchman of a ruined village. You learn to purify defeated champions
into **Covenant Echoes** and lead them as a remnant.

The game is not about becoming a god. Echoes are battle-imprints made clean,
not souls, and Elior stays human from start to finish. Scripture shown in the
game is quoted from the King James Version and always cited. Everything else
is original fiction inspired by biblical themes, not part of the Bible.

> *True greatness is not found in the power to rule, but in the strength to remain faithful.*

This build contains five chapters:

- **Chapter I — The Ashen Village.** 12 rooms, the full combat and Echo
  systems, abilities and skill trees, gear, crafting and merchants, and the
  **Gilded Idol** boss fight.
- **Chapter II — The Tower of Shinar.** Nine rooms across a dusk plain, a
  brickworks and a red tower. It adds:
  - Heavy foes (Ironbound Brutes, Shieldwall Bulwarks and Crimson Knights)
    that break into a long, juggleable stagger.
  - **The Gatekeeper**, a three-headed hound chained at the tower gate.
  - The Crimson Guard's three waves.
  - **The Crimson Marshal**, an honour duel your Echoes may not join. When
    he kneels, you choose: raise him as your strongest Echo, or let him rest
    and take his sword.
- **Chapter III — The Valley of Bones.** Ezekiel's valley, stripped bare by a
  locust swarm that has built a hive inside the bones of giants.
  - Locust drones swoop in packs, and locust soldiers fight with scythe claws.
  - **The Locust King** hatches from a cocoon in the brood chamber. He is
    blindingly fast and has claw strings, aerial dives, acid and a paralysing
    screech; he calls his swarm and devours his own drones to heal. Beaten,
    he is raised as your Echo.
  - **The Trial of the Remnant** is an endless rounds arena that keeps pace
    with your level. It is the way to level 100 and beyond.
- **Chapter IV — The Plain of Dura.** By the rivers of Babylon, after the book
  of Daniel.
  - A satrap has raised an image of gold and commands every captive to bow.
  - Three captives who would not are bound for the burning fiery furnace.
  - Pass the blue Gate of Babylon and the Processional Way. Brazen Lions
    pounce, and Chaldean Fire-Priests rain fire.
  - Drop into the Lions' Den to purify its lion as an Echo.
  - Hold the furnace mouth, then stand while the whole plain kneels.
  - **The Image of Gold:** head of gold, arms of silver, belly of brass,
    legs of iron and feet of clay. Strike its feet.
- **Chapter V — The Writing on the Wall.** Daniel 5: Babylon on the night of
  the great feast.
  - An army from the east has turned the river aside. Walk into the city
    along the dry riverbed while a thousand lords drink.
  - Cross the lantern-lit streets and climb the Hanging Gardens. Vessel
    Wraiths (stolen temple cups full of the Veil) pour burning wine, and
    Scribes of the Veil write letters that strike.
  - Find the three vessels of the temple for Hanan, an old exile scribe.
  - In the banquet hall a hand writes on the plaster: MENE, MENE, TEKEL,
    UPHARSIN.
  - **The Weigher:** the Lord of the Feast, given to the Veil, with great
    bronze scales. Three phases: Numbered, Weighed, Divided.
- Each chapter has an ending, plus New Game+.

Each chapter begins from the previous chapter's ending screen, from any
altar once the one before is done (**Journey on**), or from the
**Chapters** tab in the menu.

### Chapter select and the Trial

- **Menu → Chapters** lists every chapter with its status.
  - Travel to any chapter you have reached; you arrive at its camp altar.
  - Begin the next one once the chapter before it is complete.
- **The Trial of the Remnant** is an endless rounds mode in the spirit of
  *Call of Duty* Zombies. Enter it from **Menu → Chapters** or any altar once
  the opening is done.
  - Foes pour in from both sides, round after round. Each round brings more
    of them, with more health, harder hits and a little more speed.
  - A big blood-red counter shows the round: tally marks for rounds 1 to 5,
    then the number. It flashes when a new round begins.
  - Every fifth round **the Pack runs** (Gloom Hounds, later Brazen Lions).
    Every tenth a **Champion of the Trial** comes. Every fifth round held
    pays out silver, materials and gear.
  - Fallen foes sometimes drop a **blessing**:

    | Blessing | Effect |
    |---|---|
    | Judgment | Fire falls on every foe in the arena |
    | Wrath | Every foe falls to one blow for 18 seconds |
    | Double Portion | Twice the experience for 25 seconds |
    | Manna | Health, Balm, energy and hearts restored |

  - **Falling ends the run.** The Game Over card shows the rounds you
    survived, your kills, time and levels gained.
  - **Leaderboard:** runs that start at round 1 are ranked by the round they
    reached, on this device's board (shared by all save slots). You can put
    your name on it from the Game Over card. In the claude.ai preview there
    is also an **Everyone** board shared by all players there. Open it any
    time from **Menu → Chapters → Leaderboard**.
  - Once you have reached rounds 10, 20, 30 and so on, you can practise from
    them (unranked). Every foe pays experience scaled to your level, so the
    Trial is also the endless way to level 100 and beyond.

### Lamps, hearts and sub-weapons

A few things borrowed from *Castlevania*:

- **Lamps** hang along the walls of every room. Strike one to break it.
  They come back when you return to the room.
  - Most drop a **heart**; some drop a big heart (5) or silver.
  - Now and then one drops a **sub-weapon**. The first lamp you ever break
    always gives the Dagger.
- **Hearts** are the ammunition for sub-weapons (up to 99). They show next
  to your Balm vials.
- **Sub-weapons:** you carry one at a time. Picking up another swaps it;
  picking up the one you have gives 5 hearts.

  | Sub-weapon | Hearts | What it does |
  |---|---|---|
  | Dagger | 1 | Thrown straight and fast |
  | Axe | 2 | Thrown high in an arc; cuts through everything, and reaches foes above you |
  | Holy Water | 2 | A flask that breaks into a pool of blue fire |
  | Cross | 3 | Flies out, turns, and comes back, cutting through foes both ways (found from Chapter II on) |
  | David's Sling | 1 | A stone that knocks foes off balance |

  Throw with **Sub** on touch, **U** on a keyboard, or hold **up and press
  Heavy** on any device.
- **Map exploration:** the Map tab shows the share of rooms you have
  explored, in this chapter and in all.
- **Bestiary:** **Journal → Bestiary** records every kind of foe you have
  slain, with a field note and its base numbers, and the great foes you
  have overcome. Foes you have not met yet show as ???.

### Saving, directions and updates

- **Saving:** the game saves itself every 30 seconds, on every doorway and
  whenever you leave the app. The title screen's **Continue** shows where you
  are. If you fall, you rise again at the doorway of the room you were in.
- **Cloud copy:** in the claude.ai preview each player's saves are also kept
  in a private cloud copy.
- **Directions:** a golden marker shows the way to your next objective. An
  arrow at the screen edge points toward it when it is out of view, the map
  marks it with a star, and a hint appears if you seem lost. You can turn it
  off under **Settings → Objective marker**.
- **Updates:** the installed app checks GitHub Pages for a newer build when
  it opens or comes back to the front. When it finds one, it installs it at
  the next doorway and puts you back where you were.

### Combat highlights

- **Phantom Step:** land a hit, then dodge to blink behind the nearest foe.
  Chain blinks around a crowd; after the third, attack for **Judgment
  Cross**, a launching cross-cut.
- **New combos:** Seventy Times Seven (thrust flurry), Storm of Edges (a
  whirl that pulls foes in), Rising Comet and Heaven's Rend. A combo counter
  ranks long strings. The full move list is in **Menu → Skills**.
- **Switch support:** tag in a benched Echo mid-strike, or trade places
  with one on the field.
- **A growing army:** more Echoes can fight at once as you level: seven by
  level 26, then one more at 50, 75 and 100 (ten in all).
- **Finishers:** executing a staggered foe plays a slow-motion finisher,
  picked at random from those your level has unlocked. They are:
  - Judgment Leap.
  - Shadow Step, a teleport behind the foe and a stab through the back.
  - Cross Severance.
  - Thousand Cuts.
  - Heaven's Verdict.
  - Whirlwind Requiem.
  - Elemental Ruin.
  - Arise.

  Killing blows can also cut foes in two under a slow-motion kill cam.
- **Power that grows:** at levels 10, 20, 35, 50 and 75 your strikes get
  bigger, brighter and heavier. From 35, finishing blows send out sword
  waves.
- **Elemental weapons:** each element has its own colours and on-hit effect.
  - Fire burns.
  - Frost slows.
  - Storm chains lightning.
  - Shadow drinks life and strikes twice.
  - Light sears great foes.
  - Crimson makes foes bleed.
- **The Shadow Mantle (from level 25):** a transformation.
  - Fighting fills the Mantle gauge (under the energy bar). Hits, kills,
    parries and wounds all feed it.
  - With a full gauge, transform: the world darkens, the shadows pour in,
    then burst out and throw back everything nearby.
  - Elior wears a black hooded cloak edged in blue-violet light, with
    glowing eyes that streak light behind him. Shadow afterimages and black
    flame trail his every move.
  - While it lasts (18 seconds at first, 40 from level 100):
    - 1.5× damage, quicker swings and feet.
    - Half damage taken, and nothing staggers him.
    - Free Phantom Steps.
    - A shadow wave on every swing, and each cut drinks life and calls a
      shadow blade.
    - Fallen foes may rise as shadow soldiers, and executions can end in
      Arise.
  - It deepens at levels 50, 75 and 100 (up to 2.2× damage).
- **Level 100, the Sovereign:** a black-and-gold form with violet eyes.
  - Every swing looses a crescent.
  - Phantom Step costs nothing.
  - Ordinary blows cannot stagger you.
  - Fallen foes may rise to fight for you.

## Play it on iPhone

The game is a single self-contained web page. Once it is installed to the Home
Screen it works offline, fullscreen and in landscape.

1. Open **https://mrbobb808.github.io/Boatride/** in **Safari** on the iPhone.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**.
   Make sure **Open as Web App** is on, then tap **Add**.
3. Launch **Remnant** from the Home Screen and turn the phone sideways.

Once it has been opened, it keeps working with no connection. When a new
version is published, it updates the next time you open the app while online.

**Saves.** iOS keeps the Home Screen app's storage separate from Safari's, so
a game started in a Safari tab does not appear in the installed app. To move a
save across:

1. On the side that has the save, open **Menu → Settings → Save backup** and
   tap **Copy save code**.
2. On the other side, open **Title → Settings → Save backup**, paste the code
   and tap **Import into this slot**.

The same code works for backing up a save, or for moving it to another
device.

Tip: by default the game plays sound even when the iPhone is set to silent.
Turn on **Settings → Audio → Respect the silent switch** if you want the
switch to mute the game and leave your own music playing.

### Publishing the page (one-time setup)

The workflow in `.github/workflows/remnant-pages.yml` builds the game and
deploys it to GitHub Pages on every push to the default branch that touches
`remnant/`. To set it up:

1. In the repository, go to **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Source**
   and choose **GitHub Actions**.
2. Merge the game into the default branch, or run the workflow by hand from
   the **Actions** tab.

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| Left half of the screen | Floating stick: move. Push down while airborne and attack to plunge. |
| **Attack** (large button) | Tap for a combo; hold to charge a heavy; push up on the stick and tap for a launcher |
| **Jump** | Jump (double jump after the Plume) |
| **Dodge** | Dash with invulnerability frames; air dash |
| **Parry** | Tap just before a hit to parry; hold to block with a shield; a parry or block opens a counter |
| Skill buttons | The three equipped Covenant abilities |
| **Balm** | Heal |
| **Echoes** | Echo command wheel: summon or dismiss, Aggressive / Defensive / Follow stance, mark target, recall, sync strike, switch |
| **Switch** | Switch support: the next Echo tags in, or you trade places with one. It appears while Echoes are out. |
| **Rally** | *Rally the Remnant* (once the gauge is full) |
| **Mantle** | Transform into the *Shadow Mantle* (from level 25, once its gauge is full) |
| **Sub** | Throw your sub-weapon. The badge shows your hearts. |
| Context button | Talk, open, rest, purify. It appears only when something is in reach. |
| ☰ and map (top corner) | Menu and map |

The **Settings → Touch controls** section adjusts button size, opacity and
left-handed layout, and turns haptics on or off.

### Controller (PS5 DualSense, PS4, Xbox and others)

Pair the controller with the iPhone first: on a PS5 DualSense, hold **PS**
and **Create** until the light bar flashes, then pick it in the iPhone's
**Settings → Bluetooth**. Open the game and press any button. The game sees
which kind of pad it is and names buttons to match (✕ ○ □ △ and L1/R1 on
PlayStation, A B X Y and LB/RB on Xbox).

**Settings → Controller** shows the connection live, with left-stick values
and the last button received. Tap **Check controller** after returning from
Bluetooth settings, then press **✕ / A** or move the stick. The first press
also works when switching from touch, and unplugging or losing a connection
releases held actions and brings touch controls back on a phone.

If a preview blocks controller access, Settings explains it and offers
**Open game directly**: https://mrbobb808.github.io/Boatride/. Pairing happens
in the device's settings; the game reads the controller through the browser.
Touch and keyboard controls remain available if that access is blocked.

The stick has to point mostly up or down for a launcher or plunge, so
attacking while running diagonally stays a normal combo. The D-pad does
not move Elior; it holds quick actions, so using one never makes him step.

- **Remapping:** move any action to any button in **Settings → Controller**.
  Select an action, then press the button you want. Two actions that share
  a button swap.
- **Menus:** they always use ✕ / A to confirm and ○ / B to go back.
- **Rumble:** the controller vibrates on every hit (harder for finishers and
  crits), parries, plunges, the Mantle, boss kills and new Trial rounds,
  where the browser supports it. **Settings → Controller → Rumble → Test**
  tells you whether yours does. Safari on iPhone has not supported controller
  vibration so far (Safari on a Mac and Chrome do); the game uses it as soon
  as the browser allows.

### Boss difficulty

**Settings → Challenge → Boss difficulty** sets how hard every boss fights.
The default is **Hard**: bosses have about 30% more health, hit 25% harder
and move 10% faster. **Normal** is the original balance, and **Merciless**
goes further still. A change applies from the next boss room.

## Development

```sh
cd remnant
npm install
npm run dev        # http://localhost:5173 (also reachable from a phone on the same Wi-Fi)
npm run build      # type-checks, tests controller input, then writes dist/
npm run test:controller # controller handoff, reconnect, menu and input regressions
npm run preview    # serves dist/
```

Debug helpers are available when you add `?debug` to the URL.
`?debug&autostart` skips the title screen and starts a new game.

### How it is built

- **Code:** TypeScript and Vite, with no game engine and no image assets.
  - Characters, environments, icons and effects are all drawn procedurally on
    a Canvas 2D renderer.
  - The renderer uses skeletal animation, cloth physics, a lighting pass and
    bloom.
- **Audio:** music and sound effects are synthesized live with Web Audio.
- **Build output:** `vite-plugin-singlefile` inlines everything (code, styles
  and fonts) into one `index.html`. The manifest, service worker, icons and
  launch images are copied next to it from `public/`.
- **iPhone tuning:**
  - ProMotion 120 Hz with a fixed 120 Hz simulation step.
  - Safe-area aware HUD and controls, clear of the Dynamic Island and the
    home indicator.
  - No `shadowBlur`, which is slow in Safari.
  - Pre-baked tile chunks and pooled particles.
  - Automatic quality scaling.
  - Pixel-art mode (the default art style) draws the world at one pixel per
    world unit and scales it up hard-edged, so the world costs a fraction of
    the fill. Settings → Display → Art style switches back to Smooth.
- **Fonts:** Cinzel and Cormorant Garamond, used under the SIL Open Font
  License.

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `src/core` | Loop, math, input, settings |
| `src/render` | Renderer, camera, skeleton, characters, environment, lighting, effects |
| `src/audio` | Sound effects and the music sequencer |
| `src/game` | World, player, weapons, enemies and tanks, Echoes, bosses, rooms, story (one file per chapter), data, saves |
| `src/ui` | HUD, touch controls, menus, screens, dialogue |
