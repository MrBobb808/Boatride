# CC0 art in Remnant: Ascension

Every file under `art/incoming/cc0/` is from a pack released under **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**, as stated on its
OpenGameArt page on 6 November 2026. CC0 needs no credit; we give it anyway. The files are kept exactly as downloaded
(only the ones the game uses); `art/tools/cc0.py` turns them into the sheets under `src/assets/cc0/`.
